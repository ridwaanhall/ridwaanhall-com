{% load image_filters %} {% comment %} Profile photo with its availability dot. Rendered in three places -- the mobile navbar, the mobile drawer, and the desktop sidebar rail -- which were previously three hand-copied blocks. They had already drifted: only the navbar's dot carried the `border-2 border-zinc-950` ring, so in the two sidebars the coloured dot sat directly on the photograph instead of reading as a distinct badge. The ring is not decorative. It separates the dot from whatever pixels of the avatar happen to be behind it, and `zinc-950` tracks the page canvas in both themes (near-black on dark, near-white on light), so the dot reads as punched out of the surface rather than painted onto the photo.
